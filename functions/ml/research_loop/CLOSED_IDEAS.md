## Lessons to carry forward
- Costs dominate tight brackets: $1 RT on MES with an 8-tick stop is 0.10R (MES tick $1.25 → $10 risk); use stops wide enough that costs are < ~0.05R.
- Single-window passes have repeatedly failed the next window (BTC → NQ → ES; ORB 2022+ → 2011-21). Require two independent validation windows.
- Many "edges" were long drift in 2022+ or a trend-day regime. Compare against always-long and random-direction twins.
- Most intraday trades that miss 1R end at the flat-by-close; the close drift then decides the result.
